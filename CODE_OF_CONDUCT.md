# Code of conduct

Be respectful, direct, and constructive.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information
about another participant. Discuss the work, not the worth of the person. Give
clear technical evidence and allow good-faith correction.

Maintainers can edit or remove abusive content and can restrict participation
when conduct puts people or the project at risk. Report conduct concerns through
a private channel to the repository owner. Do not publish sensitive reports.

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, review systems, and
events where a person represents the project.
